I saw a video from Peter McKinnon come across my feed (linked below) and in it he has a quote that stuck with me. If 
you know Peter this shouldn't be a surprise, the dude is quite quotable. 

> You can always improve. Doesn't matter what peak you stand on, doesn't matter where you are in your career. There's
> always room to get better. There's always room to sharpen.

The quote itself is great, but I think for software engineers it comes at a very weird time. We are watching AI take over
a part of the job a lot of us loved: the crafting of the code and we turn into more psuedo-managers as we attempt to wrangle
the AI tools as if they are brand new junior developers. A lot of people are bemoaning the lost of craftsmenship that AI has essentially
taken, and I dont disagree.
